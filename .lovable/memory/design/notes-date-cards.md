Notes cards are white, restrained, rounded grouped rows like the supplied March screenshot. Order section headings Today, Yesterday, This Week, then months in reverse chronology; old months include the year. Show each note's date below its title and its tags when present. Use the geometric sans font style from the supplied second image both in note cards and inside the note editor. Do not change folders, header, search bar, or buttons outside the cards.
