name: Notes date cards and typography
description: Flowist Notes uses equal-height white grouped strips, Flowist-header-style sentence-case date headings, larger preview text, and no note-type icons
type: design
---
Notes cards are white, restrained, rounded grouped rows like the supplied March screenshot. Keep every note strip the same fixed height, even with tags. Order section headings Today, Yesterday, This week, then months in reverse chronology; old months include the year. Headings use the same inherited typeface and boldness as the Flowist wordmark beside the header logo; use sentence case, not all caps. Show each note's date and a slightly larger single-line content preview beneath its title, and tags when present; omit Regular/Lined and other note-type icons at the strip's end. Keep the geometric sans style in note cards and the editor. Do not change folders, header, search bar, or buttons outside the cards.
